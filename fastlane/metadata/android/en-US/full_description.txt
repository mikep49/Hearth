Hearth is a native app for CasaOS home servers. Check your server, manage your apps, browse your files and open a terminal, all from your phone.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Home:</b> live CPU, temperature, memory, network and storage, arranged and sized the way you like, with every drive's health and a Task manager showing what each app uses</li>
<li><b>Apps:</b> start, stop, restart and update apps, change their settings or version, and read their logs live</li>
<li><b>App Store:</b> browse and install apps from your server's stores</li>
<li><b>Files:</b> browse, preview, upload, download, share and search everything on the server, as a list, as thumbnails or as a photo gallery</li>
<li><b>Terminal:</b> an SSH shell with password or key sign-in, copy and paste, extra keys and a one-tap server update</li>
<li><b>Tailscale:</b> see whether you reach the server through Tailscale, with the server's Tailscale address and the other devices on your tailnet</li>
<li><b>Alerts:</b> optional notifications when a server goes offline, storage is nearly full, an app stops or an update is out</li>
<li><b>Servers:</b> save several servers and switch between them, or let Hearth find them on your Wi-Fi</li>
<li><b>Lock:</b> optional fingerprint, face or PIN lock</li>
<li><b>Widget:</b> your server's stats on the home screen</li>
<li><b>Themes:</b> system, light or dark, pure black, six accent colors, and an Android or One UI style</li>
<li><b>Tablets:</b> a side rail and wider layouts on big screens and in landscape</li>
<li><b>Home screen:</b> optional, Hearth as the home screen of a tablet kept just for your server</li>
</ul>

Sign in with your server's address (for example 192.168.1.20 or casaos.local) and the same login you use for the CasaOS dashboard.

<b>Privacy and security</b>
Hearth has no ads, analytics or tracking. It only talks to your own server, except when you pick an app's version: then it reads the list of versions from the app's image registry, such as Docker Hub, and sends nothing about you or your server.

CasaOS uses plain HTTP by default, so your password isn't encrypted on the network. Use Hearth on your home network, or connect through a VPN such as Tailscale or WireGuard when you're away. Your logins are stored encrypted on the phone, and the terminal uses SSH, which is always encrypted.

Hearth is an unofficial app and isn't affiliated with CasaOS or IceWhale.
